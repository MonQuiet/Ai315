# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a respectful and constructive environment for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Focus on evidence-based discussion.
- No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- No doxxing or sharing private/sensitive information.
- No illegal content or malicious defamation.

## Scope
This code of conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Enforcement
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please open an issue with minimal details needed to act. Maintainers will review and take appropriate action.

## Acknowledgement
This document is inspired by community best practices for open-source collaboration.
